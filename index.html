TRANSPARENCY & REPRODUCIBILITY

Methodology & Limitations

01

Satellite Observations

Monthly water extent is presented from the provided Sentinel-2 optical and Sentinel-1 radar outputs. The dashboard does not reprocess satellite imagery.

02

Rainfall Context

Rainfall and inflow are displayed from the six-month baseline CSV. They are model inputs/outputs, not a live rainfall feed.

03

Digital Twin Baseline

Storage, release and evaporation are shown as supplied scenario outputs. Placeholder assumptions may materially affect the results.

04

Known Limitations

Exact spatial boundary, forecast file and model dataset were not included in the supplied files. No GeoAI score or future forecast is displayed.

Important Use Limitation

This portfolio demonstrator is built from public-data workflows and supplied model outputs. Storage values are not measured reservoir levels. It must not be used for dam operations, flood warnings, water allocation, infrastructure safety or other operational decisions.
