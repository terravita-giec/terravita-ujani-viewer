TRANSPARENCY & REPRODUCIBILITY

Methodology & Limitations

01

Satellite Observations

Monthly water extent is presented from Sentinel-2 optical and Sentinel-1 radar outputs. The dashboard visualises processed satellite results rather than reprocessing imagery in the browser.

02

Rainfall Context

Rainfall and inflow are displayed from the six-month baseline dataset. They represent model inputs or outputs, not a live rainfall feed.

03

Digital Twin Baseline

Storage, release and evaporation are shown as supplied scenario outputs. Assumptions and placeholder parameters may materially affect the results.

04

Forecast & Model Limitations

Forecasts and GeoAI outputs depend on the supplied datasets and model assumptions. Predicted water extent is not equivalent to measured storage or a verified future reservoir condition.

Important Use Limitation

This portfolio demonstrator uses satellite-derived observations and simplified model outputs. Storage values are not measured reservoir levels. It must not be used for dam operations, flood warnings, water allocation, infrastructure safety or other operational decisions.
